Gulzar

Cook, make and draw, one step at a time, with big pictures and a voice. Every finished thing adds a keepsake to a cosy room.

On a tablet or phone

Open Gulzar
  1. Open Gulzar in Chrome.
  2. Open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen, so it opens full screen like an app.
  3. Hold the gear on the home screen for two seconds to reach the helper settings: name, language, voice, text size and colours.

On Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses

Add to my glasses Open this page on the phone paired with the glasses. The button hands Gulzar to the Meta AI app, which asks on the glasses whether to add it. To bring the tablet's settings along (name, language, voice), use Helper settings → Add to glasses in Gulzar on that phone instead.
