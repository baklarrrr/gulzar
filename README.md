# Gulzar

Cook, make and draw, one step at a time, with big pictures, very large text and a voice. Everything you finish adds a keepsake to a cosy room.

- **Open it:** https://baklarrrr.github.io/gulzar/
- **Setup:** https://baklarrrr.github.io/gulzar/install.html. On a tablet, add it to the home screen. On Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses, add it from the paired phone.

It is made for a tablet first and also works on a phone. On a 600 × 600 display such as Meta Ray-Ban Display, it switches to a layout for swipes and pinches.

## What is in it

- **Cook:** three no-cook recipes (fruit chaat, rose sharbat, kachumber salad). Each step that needs a knife says "Ask a helper".
- **Make:** a tissue-paper flower, a card for someone, and a bead bracelet.
- **Draw:** four step-by-step drawings. Draw on paper (and take a photo of it if you like) or with a finger on the screen.
- **My room:** each finished thing adds a keepsake, and your drawings hang on the wall.
- **My day:** a short daily list. Finishing it grows a plant.
- **Helper settings** (hold the gear for two seconds): a name, English or Urdu, voice, text size, colours, which activities show, and the day list.

## Privacy

Everything stays on the device, in the browser's local storage. There are no accounts, no analytics and no network requests after the page loads. A link made for the glasses carries the helper's settings in the part of the address after `#`, which browsers don't send to this site's server. The Meta AI app sees the whole link when it is added to the glasses.
